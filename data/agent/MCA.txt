# Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) - XBRL Filing Guidelines & Architecture

## 1. Overview of MCA XBRL Filing
In India, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) mandates the filing of financial statements in eXtensible Business Reporting Language (XBRL) format for eligible companies using Form AOC-4 XBRL (and Form CRA-4 for Cost Audit Reports).
XBRL is an open international standard for digital business reporting, allowing financial data to be tagged, validated, exchanged, and analyzed automatically.

## 2. Applicability Criteria for AOC-4 XBRL
Under the Companies Act, 2013 and relevant MCA notifications, the following classes of companies are required to file their financial statements in XBRL:
1. All companies listed with any Stock Exchange in India and their Indian subsidiaries.
2. All companies having paid-up capital of Rs. 5 crore and above.
3. All companies having turnover of Rs. 100 crore and above.
4. All companies which are required to prepare their financial statements in accordance with Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015 (Ind AS).
Exemptions: Banking companies, Insurance companies, Power sector companies, and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) not governed by Ind AS are generally exempt or follow specific sectoral taxonomies.

## 3. MCA XBRL Taxonomies
The MCA maintains separate taxonomies for different accounting frameworks and statutory filings:
1. **Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Taxonomy 2016 (V1.2@31-03-2016)**:
   - Applicable for non-Ind AS companies (AS / Indian GAAP).
   - Governed by Schedule III Division I of the Companies Act, 2013.
   - Covers Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit and Loss, Cash Flow Statement, Corporate Information, and Notes to Accounts.
2. **Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) Taxonomy 2017 (V1.2@31-03-2017)**:
   - Applicable for companies adopting Indian Accounting Standards converged with IFRS.
   - Governed by Schedule III Division II of the Companies Act, 2013.
   - Distinct elements for Other Comprehensive Income (OCI), Financial Instruments, Leases, and Fair Value measurements.
3. **Costing Taxonomy 2019 (Version 1.1@31-03-2019)**:
   - Applicable for filing Cost Audit Reports and Compliance Reports via Form CRA-4.
   - Includes production statistics, cost of sales, cost of materials consumed, and reconciliation with financial records.

## 4. Key Tagging Principles & Concepts
- **Concept QName**: Every reporting concept has a unique Qualified Name consisting of a prefix and a local element name (e.g., `in-gaap:CashAndCashEquivalents`, `ind-as:RevenueFromOperations`).
- **Period Types**:
  - `instant`: Represents financial position at a single point in time (e.g., Balance Sheet items as of March 31).
  - `duration`: Represents financial activity over a period of time (e.g., Profit & Loss line items, Cash Flow movements from April 1 to March 31).
- **Balance Type**:
  - `debit`: Typical for assets, expenses, losses.
  - `credit`: Typical for liabilities, equity, revenue, gains.
- **Reporting Scope**:
  - Current Year (CY) and Previous Year (PY) comparisons.
  - Consolidated vs. Standalone filings.
- **Dimensional Modeling & Tables**:
  - Dimensional hypercubes define multi-axis tables (e.g., Share Capital classes, Related Party disclosures, Segment reporting).
  - Explicit members and typed dimensions ensure data points are uniquely identified by their axis combinations.

## 5. MCA Validation Rules & Error Handling
The MCA Validation Tool applies strict deterministic business rules before an instance document (`.xml`) can be pre-scrutinized and uploaded:
1. **Mandatory Non-Null Items**: Key demographic and financial elements must not be empty or nil (e.g., CIN, Company Name, Balance Sheet dates, Auditor Details, Signatory Details).
2. **Arithmetic & Continuity Equations**:
   - Total Assets = Total Equity and Liabilities.
   - Net Profit after Tax = Profit Before Tax - Tax Expense.
   - Opening Cash + Net Cash Inflow/Outflow = Closing Cash.
3. **Period Consistency**: Dates across contexts must strictly align with the reporting financial year and prior year.
4. **Sign Conventions**: Negative figures should only be reported where the element documentation explicitly permits debit/credit reversal or negative values.
